# MAESTRO Sovereign Intelligence Constitution

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-01
MAESTRO SHALL HAVE NO CONSTITUTIONAL DEPENDENCY ON ANY MODEL, LLM, PROVIDER, MODEL FAMILY OR INFERENCE RUNTIME.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-02
A MODEL MAY PROVIDE INTELLIGENCE TO MAESTRO, BUT SHALL NEVER OWN MAESTRO'S IDENTITY, AUTHORITY, MEMORY, GOVERNANCE OR CONTINUITY.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-03
MAESTRO SHALL BE ABLE TO DISCOVER, EVALUATE, BENCHMARK, SELECT, COMBINE, REPLACE AND ABANDON INTELLIGENCE RESOURCES AT RUNTIME.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-04
MODEL FAILURE SHALL DEGRADE CAPABILITY, NOT DESTROY MAESTRO.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-05
NO MODEL MAY OWN:
- mission lifecycle;
- constitutional authority;
- durable memory;
- tool permissions;
- Council authority;
- policy activation;
- TX commit;
- human approval.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-06
AN INTELLIGENCE RESOURCE RECEIVES AN INFERENCE CONTRACT, NOT SYSTEM AUTHORITY.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-07
CAPABILITY IS SELECTED BEFORE PROVIDER OR MODEL IDENTITY.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-08
LOCAL-FIRST IS AN OPTIMIZATION POLICY, NOT A CONSTITUTIONAL DEPENDENCY.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-09
MULTI-MODEL AGREEMENT IS EVIDENCE, NOT TRUTH.

## APTE-SOVEREIGN-10
MAESTRO MAY OPERATE IN DETERMINISTIC DEGRADED MODE WITH ZERO LLMs AVAILABLE.
