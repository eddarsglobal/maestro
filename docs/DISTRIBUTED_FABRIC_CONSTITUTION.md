# Distributed Fabric Constitution

## FAB-01
Distribution changes placement, not constitutional authority.
## FAB-02
A lease grants temporary execution ownership, not Council jurisdiction.
## FAB-03
Expired work may be reclaimed; protected external actions require idempotency at the action boundary.
## FAB-04
Idempotency keys cannot be reused for materially different requests.
## FAB-05
Distributed locks are coordination aids, not substitutes for domain invariants or database constraints.
## FAB-06
Worker capability advertisements are claims; sensitive placement may require validated worker attestation in future versions.
## FAB-07
Context references are immutable content-addressed objects.
## FAB-08
Cross-node recovery must not invent success for incomplete work.
## FAB-09
Academy online ingestion remains observational and cannot self-promote policy.
## FAB-10
Skill graph optimization may remove only provably redundant dependencies unless independence is explicitly declared and validated.
