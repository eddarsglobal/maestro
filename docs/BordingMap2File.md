## Mission

Transform MAESTRO from a constitutionally complete governed Core into a fully integrated Council-native autonomous system capable of accepting a mission, determining what intelligence and capabilities are required, constructing a governed execution graph, selecting replaceable resources, executing under explicit authority, collecting evidence, recovering from failure, closing the mission and improving from experience without surrendering sovereignty to any model, provider, runtime, tool or Council.

MAESTRO COMMANDS MODELS.

MODELS DO NOT COMMAND MAESTRO.

MAESTRO SHALL REMAIN INDEPENDENT OF ANY SPECIFIC MODEL, PROVIDER, RUNTIME, HARDWARE CLASS, PROTOCOL OR CONTEMPORARY TECHNOLOGY IDENTITY.

CAPABILITY CONTRACTS ARE CONSTITUTIONAL.

## BordingMap 2.0 points

BM2-001 | EVERGREEN | sovereignty | MAESTRO identity remains independent of models, providers, runtimes, hardware classes and contemporary technology identities.

BM2-002 | EVERGREEN | authority | Autonomous planning, routing, research, learning and recovery never bypass EX, AY, OP, SC, TX or other constitutional authority boundaries.

BM2-003 | EVERGREEN | zero-model | MAESTRO remains bootable and constitutionally coherent without any intelligence resource being available.

BM2-004 | EVERGREEN | objective | Autonomous adaptation may change method, resources, routing and strategy but never silently replace the user's mission objective.

BM2-005 | PROJECT | mission-contract | User intent is transformed into an explicit governed mission contract with objective, scope, requested output, constraints, acceptance criteria, authority requirements and evidence obligations.

BM2-006 | PROJECT | mission-analysis | MAESTRO derives an explicit mission capability demand model without invoking or selecting a specific provider merely because that provider exists.

BM2-007 | PROJECT | council-synthesis | Mission requirements deterministically produce the minimal required Council graph, preserving Council authority ownership and ultra-light lazy residency.

BM2-008 | PROJECT | council-gap | Missing Council capabilities produce governed Council-gap evidence and optional dynamic Council proposals without installation, activation or execution authority.

BM2-009 | PROJECT | dynamic-council | Dynamic Council lifecycle is complete from proposal through evaluation, shadow operation, AY validation, EX authorization and explicitly governed installation while Genesis Councils remain immutable.

BM2-010 | PROJECT | capability-graph | Mission capability requirements map to generic capability contracts independently of concrete tools, models, providers or runtimes.

BM2-011 | PROJECT | intelligence-market | Replaceable intelligence resources are discovered, characterized, ranked and selected by capabilities, evidence, policy, cost, latency, locality, privacy, reliability and mission constraints.

BM2-012 | PROJECT | model-sovereignty | Resource selection can switch models, providers or local runtimes without changing MAESTRO mission semantics, Council authority or constitutional identity.

BM2-013 | PROJECT | plan-synthesis | MAESTRO constructs a deterministic governed mission DAG containing Council ownership, dependencies, acceptance evidence, retry policy, effect requirements and recovery boundaries.

BM2-014 | PROJECT | plan-validation | AY and relevant Councils validate mission acceptance criteria and plan invariants before execution becomes eligible.

BM2-015 | PROJECT | effect-budget | Every executable mission step declares effects, permissions, resource budgets and sandbox requirements before execution.

BM2-016 | PROJECT | execution-routing | Eligible DAG nodes route through capability-aware Council execution without allowing resource adapters, tools or models to acquire mission authority.

BM2-017 | PROJECT | parallelism | Independent Council work can execute concurrently while preserving deterministic evidence lineage, authority isolation and bounded resource use.

BM2-018 | PROJECT | distributed-runtime | Mission execution can use leased local or distributed workers with ownership, expiry, duplicate-execution protection and recovery semantics.

BM2-019 | PROJECT | adaptive-routing | Runtime failures, degraded resources and capability loss trigger governed rerouting or replanning without silently changing the mission objective or authority model.

BM2-020 | PROJECT | evidence-closure | Every completed mission step binds outputs, provenance, execution evidence, relevant Council verdicts and acceptance evidence before downstream completion.

BM2-021 | PROJECT | mission-verdict | Mission completion requires explicit acceptance evidence and governed verdicts; execution success alone never implies mission success.

BM2-022 | PROJECT | durable-mission | Mission, DAG, routing, evidence, retries, Council state and required recovery information survive governed checkpoint/resume without semantic drift.

BM2-023 | PROJECT | autonomous-recovery | Mission-level failure containment, rollback, recovery and replanning compose existing recovery primitives without bypassing downstream AY/EX execution authority.

BM2-024 | PROJECT | experience-capture | Mission outcomes, failures, retries, routing quality, resource performance, user corrections and verified results produce immutable experience evidence.

BM2-025 | PROJECT | learning-boundary | Experience evidence may generate governed learning candidates but cannot directly mutate policy, memory authority, routing authority, Council authority or Core semantics.

BM2-026 | PROJECT | promotion | Learned candidates require explicit evaluation, comparison, rollback capability and governed promotion before affecting production behavior.

BM2-027 | PROJECT | radar-loop | Technology Radar observations can become research evidence, capability candidates and integration proposals while DISCOVER != TRUST != INSTALL != ACTIVATE != AUTHORIZE.

BM2-028 | PROJECT | capability-evolution | Missing capabilities may produce governed adapter, integration or architecture-evolution proposals without silently changing MAESTRO ontology or constitutional identity.

BM2-029 | PROJECT | cost-performance | Mission resource selection and execution expose measurable cost, latency, energy, locality, reliability and outcome evidence without converting optimization evidence into authority.

BM2-030 | PROJECT | privacy-locality | Mission planning can explicitly prefer local/offline/private execution and fail closed when mission privacy or locality constraints cannot be satisfied.

BM2-031 | PROJECT | autonomous-loop | MAESTRO completes an end-to-end Council-native autonomous mission loop from mission contract through planning, resource selection, governed execution, evidence, verdict, recovery and durable experience.

BM2-032 | PROJECT | sovereignty-certification | Full autonomous operation remains provider-independent, model-independent, Council-governed, evidence-bound, fail-closed, recoverable and compatible with zero-model Core sovereignty.
