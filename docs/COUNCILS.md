# MAESTRO Councils

MAESTRO Councils are governed domains, not merely conversational personas.

A Council represents a responsibility boundary, authority boundary, expected evidence and domain-specific validation logic.

The canonical public blueprints are stored in [`../blueprints/`](../blueprints/).

## Important authority examples

- EX owns dispatch and lifecycle.
- AY owns acceptance contracts and final validation.
- IT owns implementation.
- SC owns security architecture/audit.
- JR owns legal interpretation.
- TX owns durable traceability.
- PM owns product scope.
- PR owns performance/reality evidence.
- AI owns product-level AI architecture and evaluation.
- RX owns external research.

MAESTRO is designed so that one Council does not silently absorb the jurisdiction of another.
