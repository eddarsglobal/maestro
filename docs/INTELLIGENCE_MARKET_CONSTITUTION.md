# MAESTRO Intelligence Market Constitution

## APTE-MARKET-01
ELIGIBILITY PRECEDES COMPETITION.

Only intelligence resources already authorized by MAESTRO's Sovereign Intelligence Layer may enter the market.

## APTE-MARKET-02
MARKET SCORE IS NOT AUTHORITY.

A high-performing model gains preference, not Council power, tool permissions, memory ownership, policy activation rights or constitutional authority.

## APTE-MARKET-03
NO PERMANENT MODEL MONOPOLY.

MAESTRO must preserve exploration and alternative resources to avoid provider/model lock-in.

## APTE-MARKET-04
PERFORMANCE IS TASK- AND COUNCIL-SPECIFIC.

A model that is excellent for code may be poor for legal analysis, validation, research or lightweight routing.

## APTE-MARKET-05
OBSERVATION MUST BE GROUNDED.

Quality, latency, cost, reliability and success observations must be derived from real or Academy-evaluated outcomes.

## APTE-MARKET-06
ONE BENCHMARK DOES NOT CREATE A PERMANENT RULE.

Preference requires repeated evidence and remains reversible.

## APTE-MARKET-07
LOCALITY IS A PREFERENCE SIGNAL, NOT A CONSTITUTIONAL REQUIREMENT.

## APTE-MARKET-08
RESOURCE REPLACEMENT MUST PRESERVE MISSION CONTINUITY.

## APTE-MARKET-09
ACADEMY MAY GENERATE BENCHMARKS; IT MAY NOT DIRECTLY ACTIVATE A NEW CONSTITUTIONAL POLICY.

## APTE-MARKET-10
MAESTRO REMAINS THE BUYER OF INTELLIGENCE. INTELLIGENCE RESOURCES ARE SELLERS OF CAPABILITY.
