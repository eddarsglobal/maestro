# MAESTRO Governed Capability Candidate Constitution

## Scope

This constitution governs BMF-019:

MAESTRO can derive governed capability candidates from newly researched technologies.

The boundary remains passive, evidence-linked, deterministic, fail-closed, and non-authoritative.

## GCD-01 — Research precedes capability candidacy

A governed capability candidate SHALL arise only from already researched technology evidence.

A pre-research discovery candidate SHALL NOT be treated as a BMF-019 capability candidate.

RESEARCH INGRESS != RESEARCHED CAPABILITY CANDIDATE.

## GCD-02 — Explicit landscape change is required

Capability candidacy SHALL require explicit CHANGED technology-landscape evidence.

UNKNOWN SHALL produce no capability candidate.

UNCHANGED SHALL produce no capability candidate.

Missing change evidence SHALL NOT be interpreted as change.

## GCD-03 — Only current added or modified subjects may produce candidates

Capability candidates SHALL be derived only from subjects explicitly identified as added or modified in the current landscape.

Removed subjects SHALL NOT produce capability candidates.

Unchanged subjects SHALL NOT produce capability candidates.

## GCD-04 — Capability evidence must preserve lineage

Each candidate SHALL preserve:

- landscape change identity
- current snapshot identity
- scope identity
- technology subject identity
- research evidence identity
- source reference
- complete landscape evidence lineage

Foreign or ambiguous evidence SHALL fail closed.

## GCD-05 — Capability semantics must be explicit

A candidate SHALL identify a concrete capability term supported by existing governed capability-integration evaluation.

A candidate SHALL NOT invent capability semantics absent from the supplied research evidence and evaluation.

## GCD-06 — Candidate identity is deterministic

Equivalent governed inputs SHALL produce equivalent candidate identities.

Input ordering SHALL NOT fabricate different semantic candidates.

Candidate ordering SHALL be deterministic.

## GCD-07 — External evidence remains unverified

Capability candidates derived from external research SHALL remain:

UNVERIFIED_EXTERNAL

Capability candidacy SHALL NOT assert system truth.

CANDIDATE != VERIFIED FACT.

## GCD-08 — Capability candidacy carries no downstream authority

CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != TRUST
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != INTEGRATION CANDIDATE
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != ADAPTER CANDIDATE
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != INSTALL
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != ACTIVATE
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != AUTHORIZE
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != EXECUTE
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != CORE MUTATION
CAPABILITY CANDIDATE != SYSTEM TRUTH

Any attempted escalation SHALL fail closed.

## GCD-09 — BMF-019 remains separate from BMF-020

BMF-019 derives governed capability candidates.

BMF-020 may later propose integration or adapter candidates.

BMF-019 SHALL NOT:

- register an adapter
- create an integration proposal
- create an adapter proposal
- bind a provider
- activate a provider
- authorize execution

CAPABILITY DERIVATION != INTEGRATION PROPOSAL.

## GCD-10 — Derivation remains passive and technology-generic

The governed candidate derivation boundary SHALL perform no:

- network access
- external research
- process launch
- persistence
- learning
- promotion
- installation
- activation
- execution
- provider invocation
- Core mutation

Provider, model, runtime, hardware, protocol, and operating-system identities remain external data rather than constitutional identities.

## Canonical boundary

RESEARCHED TECHNOLOGY EVIDENCE
        +
EXPLICIT LANDSCAPE CHANGE
        +
CURRENT LANDSCAPE SNAPSHOT
        +
CAPABILITY INTEGRATION EVALUATION
        |
        v
GOVERNED CAPABILITY CANDIDATE

## Final invariant

MAESTRO MAY IDENTIFY A POSSIBLE NEW CAPABILITY WITHOUT DECIDING TO INTEGRATE, TRUST, INSTALL, ACTIVATE, AUTHORIZE, EXECUTE, OR MUTATE ANYTHING.
