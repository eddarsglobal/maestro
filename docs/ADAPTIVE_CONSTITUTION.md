# MAESTRO Adaptive Constitution

## ADAPT-01 — Active policies are immutable versions
MAESTRO never edits an active policy in place. It creates a newer candidate version.

## ADAPT-02 — Learning cannot self-promote
Learning and Academy may generate, evaluate and recommend policy candidates only.

## ADAPT-03 — Shadow before active
Every learned policy must pass through SHADOW before production activation.

## ADAPT-04 — Replay regression is explicit
A candidate with unacceptable historical regression cannot be promoted because its average score is higher.

## ADAPT-05 — AY validates promotion evidence
AY validates that the candidate satisfies the promotion contract.

## ADAPT-06 — EX authorizes activation
Only EX may authorize the active policy transition.

## ADAPT-07 — Rollback is first-class
Every activation retains a previous policy pointer. EX can perform governed rollback with AY acknowledgement.

## ADAPT-08 — Model providers are replaceable resources
Model-selection policy may learn which model works best for a Council/task family, but no provider becomes architectural authority.

## ADAPT-09 — Tool permissions remain outside learning
Tool selection may rank permitted tools. It cannot expand the Tool Registry permissions or Action Firewall.

## ADAPT-10 — Context learning cannot remove constitutional context
Context policy may optimize budget and evidence selection. It may not remove mandatory authority/constraint rules.

## ADAPT-11 — Retry learning cannot legalize blind retry
Retry strategy learns which alternative strategies work. OP loop detection and EX/RX breakout remain constitutional.

## ADAPT-12 — Skill is not authority
A generated skill packages reusable procedure. It does not become a new Council authority.

## ADAPT-13 — Archetype is an abstraction, not truth
Discovered mission archetypes are hypotheses backed by experience IDs and remain revisable.

## ADAPT-14 — Lightness applies to adaptation
Policy histories, observations and skill corpora should be binary/reference-first; only active working policy remains hot.
