# MAESTRO ACADEMY Constitution

## ACAD-01 — Academy is not production authority
ACADEMY may evaluate, rank, simulate and recommend. It may not activate production policy.

## ACAD-02 — Training and production are isolated
Scenario execution must not create unauthorized real-world side effects.

## ACAD-03 — Historical replay is immutable
A policy candidate is evaluated against the same replay corpus as its baseline.

## ACAD-04 — Regression is first-class
Average improvement does not erase a critical regression.

## ACAD-05 — Weaknesses must be measured
MAESTRO may create a curriculum only from evidence-backed weakness signals.

## ACAD-06 — Council benchmark is jurisdiction-aware
A Council is penalized for being absent when required and for appearing when explicitly forbidden.

## ACAD-07 — Tournament winner is only a recommendation
Shadow tournament ranking cannot bypass Learning Promotion Gate, AY validation or EX authorization.

## ACAD-08 — Academy data follows Lightness Constitution
Scenarios and results may be stored in compressed binary form and loaded by reference.

## ACAD-09 — Curriculum is adaptive but bounded
ACADEMY focuses training on weak areas while preserving regression coverage of strong areas.

## ACAD-10 — No benchmark gaming
Metrics are versioned; policy changes may not redefine the benchmark after seeing results.
