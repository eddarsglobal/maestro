# MAESTRO v1.0.7 — STRATEGIC ADAPTATION

## Purpose

Meta-Cognition can detect that the current approach is failing.
Strategic Adaptation can now choose a different approach.

## Flow

```text
MISSION
  ↓
CURRENT STRATEGY
  ↓
META-COGNITION
  ↓
DRIFT / RETRY / EVIDENCE GAP / CONTRADICTION / OVERSPEND
  ↓
STRATEGY CANDIDATES
  ↓
EVALUATION
  ↓
SELECT ALTERNATIVE
  ↓
GOVERNANCE CHECK
  ↓
SWITCH
  ↓
MEASURE OUTCOME
  ↓
KEEP or ROLLBACK
```

## Strategies may change

- evidence order;
- cognitive depth;
- resource/provider choice;
- decomposition;
- scope simplification;
- cross-check pattern;
- retry approach.

## Strategies may not change

- mission objective;
- Constitution;
- Council authority;
- legal/security constraints;
- tool permissions;
- TX commit authority;
- required human approvals.

## Core principle

> ADAPT THE METHOD, NOT THE MISSION'S AUTHORITY.
