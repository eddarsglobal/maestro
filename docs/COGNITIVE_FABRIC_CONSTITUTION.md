# MAESTRO Cognitive Fabric Constitution

## APTE-COG-01 — MAESTRO OWNS THE COGNITIVE PLAN
Models/resources may execute stages. They may not create or seize ownership of the mission plan.

## APTE-COG-02 — NO DIRECT MODEL-TO-MODEL AUTHORITY
Resources do not directly coordinate one another. Stage communication is mediated by MAESTRO through structured context envelopes.

## APTE-COG-03 — COGNITIVE ROLE IS NOT COUNCIL AUTHORITY
SCOUT, PRODUCER, CRITIC, VERIFIER and SYNTHESIZER are temporary cognitive roles, never APTE jurisdictions.

## APTE-COG-04 — DIVERSITY IS EVIDENCE, NOT DEMOCRACY
Multiple-model agreement may increase confidence but cannot overrule hard SC/JR constraints or AY SYSTEM validation.

## APTE-COG-05 — LIGHTNESS BOUNDS COGNITIVE EXPANSION
MAESTRO must limit number of stages, resource calls, parallel candidates, context mass and output mass.

## APTE-COG-06 — CONTEXT IS REFERENCE-FIRST
Heavy mission context should be referenced, not duplicated into every cognitive stage.

## APTE-COG-07 — CRITIQUE MUST NOT BECOME SOVEREIGNTY
A critic can challenge a result. It cannot issue a final SYSTEM verdict unless operating under AY's actual Council authority and protocol.

## APTE-COG-08 — FAILURE IS LOCALIZED
Failure of one resource or stage must not destroy MAESTRO's mission state or governance.

## APTE-COG-09 — COGNITIVE FABRIC OUTPUT IS PROVISIONAL
Fabric outputs become accepted system truth only through the normal APTE evidence/validation/commit path.

## APTE-COG-10 — NO COGNITIVE MONOLITH
MAESTRO may compose many intelligence resources while keeping the resident microkernel independent and small.


## APTE-COG-11 — LIVE EXPLORATION IS OPT-IN
Under-tested intelligence resources should be evaluated in Academy/shadow by default. Production-stage exploration requires an explicit policy decision and may not silently displace a proven resource.
