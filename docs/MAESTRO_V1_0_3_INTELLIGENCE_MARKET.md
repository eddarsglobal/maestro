# MAESTRO v1.0.3 — INTELLIGENCE MARKET

## Purpose

MAESTRO can now treat intelligence resources as a competitive market.

The market does not ask:
"Which model is best?"

It asks:
"Which eligible resource has proven best for this task family, Council, context, risk and operating constraint?"

## Flow

```text
MISSION
  ↓
REQUIRED CAPABILITY
  ↓
SOVEREIGN ELIGIBILITY
  ↓
INTELLIGENCE MARKET
  ↓
TASK/COUNCIL SCORECARDS
  ↓
EXPLORE or EXPLOIT
  ↓
SELECT RESOURCE
  ↓
EXECUTE
  ↓
EVALUATE OUTCOME
  ↓
MARKET OBSERVATION
  ↓
UPDATED SCORECARD
```

## Market dimensions

- quality
- success rate
- reliability
- latency
- cost
- context fit
- tool fit
- locality

## Important

The market never bypasses:
- APTE Constitution
- EX
- AY
- Council authority
- tool permissions
- human approvals

A resource can win work.
It cannot win sovereignty.
