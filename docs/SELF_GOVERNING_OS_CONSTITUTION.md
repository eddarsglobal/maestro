# MAESTRO Self-Governing OS Constitution

## OS-01
Operational self-governance is subordinate to APTE constitutional authority.

## OS-02
Event history is append-only; projections may be rebuilt.

## OS-03
A worker lease expires unless heartbeated.

## OS-04
Expired work may be reclaimed; duplicate irreversible execution must still be prevented at the tool/action layer.

## OS-05
Automatic checkpoints never imply validation or TX durable truth.

## OS-06
OP may detect loops continuously but may only recommend breakout to EX.

## OS-07
Online Academy feedback is observation, not active policy mutation.

## OS-08
Council HOT/WARM/COLD state affects residency only, never jurisdiction.

## OS-09
Skill DAG compilation reuses approved Council authority; skills cannot mint new authority.

## OS-10
Async execution may parallelize independent work but cannot violate DAG dependencies.
